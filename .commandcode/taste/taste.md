# Taste

## Workflow
- For large, sweeping work (a full audit/refactor/release pass), wants a written plan produced and reviewed before any edits are made — not just a to-do list after starting. Confidence: 0.5

## Writing / content style
- Jokes and flavor text should not explain the punchline — trust the reader and never spell out the joke. Applies to all jokes in a project, not just one level/line. Confidence: 0.75

## Attribution / credits
- Keep AI-use disclosures tool-agnostic: never enumerate which AI tools/models were used (he uses many), and strip "made with AI" promo framing, but always retain the minimum disclaimer the platform/jam rules require. Confidence: 0.6
- Don't credit the AI as an author ("built with AI assistance (Claude)", "built with AI to his design document") on any shipped surface — in-game credits, README, store description, docs, social/draft copy. Keep factual AI-use disclosures where rules require them; stripping credits is not the same as stripping disclosure. Confidence: 0.75
